Distance & level of detail

The bar is the distance axis and the ticks are the real boundaries — a pair per changeover, because going out and coming back happen at different distances. Sweep and watch: the level always changes on the far tick of a pair leaving and the near tick returning, and never inside the gap.

Citizens switch on DISTANCE (07-citizens), structures on APPARENT SIZE (03-geometry) — which is why a vault holds its detail to 244m while a brazier is coarse by 21m and gone by 179m. Both rules are imported, not copied, so this page cannot disagree with the game. A structure has a third boundary the citizens do not: the cull, where it stops drawing.
